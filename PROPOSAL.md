# Tanzania Biashara Festival (TBF) — Official Proposal & Platform Documentation

- **Motto:** *"Every business has a place, every city has an opportunity."*
- **Vision:** To be Tanzania's premier platform for business, innovation, and investment opportunities.
- **Mission:** To create an inclusive platform that connects people, businesses, and investors through opportunities that drive sustainable economic growth.
