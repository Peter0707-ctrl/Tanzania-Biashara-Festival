1. Executive Summary

Tanzania Biashara Festival (TBF) is a nationwide roving trade exhibition, open-air factory auction, innovation showcase, and investment forum touring across all 31 regions of Tanzania — including Mwanza, Dar es Salaam, Arusha, Dodoma, Mbeya, and Zanzibar.

Unlike traditional static single-city trade fairs held once a year, Tanzania Biashara Festival brings commercial exhibitions, B2B matchmaking, regulatory clinics, and factory-direct open auctions directly to regional economic hubs. Every participating business receives both a physical exhibition booth on the fairgrounds and year-round digital showcase visibility on biasharafestival.co.tz.

2. Vision & Mission

Our Vision: To be Tanzania's premier platform for business, innovation, and investment opportunities.

Our Mission: To create an inclusive platform that connects people, businesses, and investors through opportunities that drive sustainable economic growth across every region of Tanzania.

3. Festival Highlights & Fairgrounds Experience

4. Core Strategic Pillars

  • Businesses First (Digital & Physical Showcase): Every registered exhibitor is featured directly on the official festival portal alongside their product portfolio, participating tour cities, booth number, and direct buyer contact line.
  • One-Stop Business & Regulatory Clinic: On-site advisory and registration desks operated in collaboration with business agencies (BRELA, TRA, TBS, TMDA, SIDO), commercial banks, and enterprise financiers.
  • Open Factory Auctions & Regional Distribution: Manufacturers, agro-processors, and importers connect directly with regional wholesalers, retail chains, and public shoppers at factory-direct prices.
  • Real-Time Interactive Booth Booking & QR Entry Pass: Exhibitors select their preferred booth slot on the interactive fairground map at biasharafestival.co.tz/booking and receive an instant verified QR Exhibitor Pass.
