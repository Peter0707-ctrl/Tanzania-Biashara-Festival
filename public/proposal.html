01. Executive Concept • Dhana Kuu ya Mradi

Biashara Kwanza & Mzunguko wa Mikoa (Roving Regional Trade Fair)

Kiswahili: Tofauti na maonyesho yanayofanyika mkoa mmoja pekee mara moja kwa mwaka, Tanzania Biashara Festival (TBF) inazunguka mkoa kwa mkoa (*Dar es Salaam, Mwanza, Arusha, Dodoma, Mbeya, Zanzibar, n.k.*) kupeleka fursa za biashara, masoko, na uwekezaji moja kwa moja kwa wananchi. Kwenye mfumo wetu wa kidijitali, Biashara Zinaonekana Kwanza (Businesses First) ili kila mfanyabiashara anayejisajili apate wateja mtandaoni na uwanjani.

English: Unlike static single-city exhibitions, Tanzania Biashara Festival (TBF) tours across Tanzania's economic regions. Our digital platform puts Showcased Businesses First, giving every registered vendor continuous online visibility, commercial advertisement space, and physical exhibition booths as the tour moves from city to city.
