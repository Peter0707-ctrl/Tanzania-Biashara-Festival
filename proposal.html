1. Utangulizi & Dhana Kuu (Executive Summary)

Tanzania Biashara Festival (TBF) ni jukwaa kubwa la kitaifa la maonyesho ya biashara, minada ya wazi ya bei za kiwandani, ubunifu, na uwekezaji linalozunguka mkoa kwa mkoa nchini Tanzania (Dar es Salaam, Mwanza, Arusha, Dodoma, Mbeya, Zanzibar na mikoa yote 31 ya Tanzania).

Unlike static single-city trade fairs held once a year, Tanzania Biashara Festival takes trade, investment forums, and factory-direct open auctions directly to regional commercial hubs. Every registered exhibitor receives both a physical exhibition booth on the fairgrounds and continuous digital visibility on biasharafestival.co.tz.

2. Dira na Dhamira Yetu (Vision & Mission)

Dira Yetu (Our Vision): Kuwa jukwaa kuu na bora zaidi nchini Tanzania kwa fursa za biashara, ubunifu, na uwekezaji (To be Tanzania's premier platform for business, innovation, and investment opportunities).

Dhamira Yetu (Our Mission): Kutengeneza jukwaa jumuishi linalounganisha wananchi, wafanyabiashara, na wawekezaji kupitia fursa zinazochochea ukuaji endelevu wa uchumi katika kila mkoa (To create an inclusive platform that connects people, businesses, and investors through opportunities that drive sustainable economic growth).

4. Nguzo Kuu za Tanzania Biashara Festival (Core Pillars)

  • Biashara Kwanza (Showcased Exhibitors First): Kila kampuni au mjasiriamali anayehifadhi banda anaonekana mubashara kwenye ukurasa wa mbele wa tovuti pamoja na bidhaa zake, mikoa anayoshiriki, na namba ya simu.
  • One-Stop Business & Regulatory Clinic: Huduma za papo kwa papo uwanjani kutoka mamlaka za usajili na viwango (BRELA, TRA, TBS, TMDA, SIDO) pamoja na madawati ya mikopo na huduma za kibenki.
  • Minada ya Wazi ya Bei za Kiwandani (Open Factory Auctions): Wananchi na wafanyabiashara wa jumla wananunua bidhaa moja kwa moja kutoka kwa wazalishaji kwa punguzo maalum la tamasha.
  • Mfumo wa Kidijitali wa Kuhifadhi Mabanda & Selcom Pay: Wafanyabiashara wanachagua namba ya banda kwenye ramani ya uwanja (`biasharafestival.co.tz/booking`) na kulipia papo hapo kwa M-Pesa, Mixx by Yas, Airtel Money, HaloPesa au Benki kupitia Selcom na kupata TRA Receipt & QR Pass.
